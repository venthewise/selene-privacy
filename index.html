Selene · Night Observatory

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Selene is an astrology, tarot, and personal reflection app. This policy explains what information the app stores on your device, what is sent over the network when you ask for a reading, and how your choices work.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

  • Birth charts & profiles. Birth dates, times, locations, saved natal charts, and compatibility (Match) profiles are stored locally in app-private storage on your phone.
  • Readings, tarot draws & journal notes. Your saved chart readings, daily tarot draws, spread pulls, and personal journal notes remain on your device in app-private storage.
  • Preferences. App settings such as theme, selected Selene voice, and tarot deck style are stored locally on your device.

2. Information Sent to Generate Readings

  • Questions & chart context. When you ask Selene a question or request an AI-generated natal, transit, or compatibility reading, the app sends your prompt and the relevant calculated chart context over HTTPS to the Selene backend proxy, which forwards the request to our hosted AI model provider to generate your reply. We do not store your conversation messages or chart readings on our servers.
  • Anonymous install & rate-limit counters. A random install identifier is generated on first launch and used strictly to enforce weekly free-tier reply limits and prevent abuse. It is not linked to your name, email, or advertising identity.

3. Subscriptions & Payments

Premium subscriptions are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. Selene receives confirmation of your active subscription entitlement and purchase token to unlock Premium features, and never receives or stores your payment card details.

4. Microphone & Voice Input

If you choose to use voice input, Selene requests Android's microphone permission solely to convert your speech into text using your device's speech recognition service. Selene does not record, upload, or store audio files.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Tracking

  • Hosted AI model provider: Receives only the active question and chart context needed to generate your reply.
  • Google Play: Processes subscription billing and entitlement verification.
  • No ads or trackers: Selene contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics trackers, and never sells your personal data.

6. Data Retention & Your Choices

  • Local data: Saved charts, tarot journal notes, and readings stay on your device until you edit or delete them in the app, or uninstall Selene.
  • Server data: Conversation content is not retained on our servers after your reply is delivered. Weekly free-tier usage counters are temporary and used only for rate limiting.
  • Permissions & subscriptions: You can revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings and manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

7. Children's Privacy

Selene is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your region), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "Last updated" date above reflects the latest version. For questions about this policy or data deletion, contact ventechstudios@gmail.com.
